# TikTok story: promote the YouTube channel

For TikTok @artjenny91_ (56.8K followers, 1.8M likes on 2026-10-28) → YouTube @jennydrawsyou.

## Before posting
- **Upload 5-10 Shorts to YouTube first.** On 2026-10-28 the channel had no videos. Fans who arrive at an
  empty channel leave and rarely come back.
- **Add YouTube to the TikTok profile:** TikTok → Profile → Edit profile → YouTube → sign in and pick the
  artjenny channel. The YouTube icon then shows on her profile; frame 3 tells people to tap it.
- **Frame 4 promises "Subscribe & I might draw you".** Only post it if she will really draw a subscriber
  (kit idea #26). Otherwise skip frame 4 or change the line to "Subscribe for a new drawing every day".
