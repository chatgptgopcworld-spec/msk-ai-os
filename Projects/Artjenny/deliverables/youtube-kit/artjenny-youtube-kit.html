What works

Why her videos travel

The format is already proven

Every top Reel is the same shot: the subject sits behind her board, her hand and marker fill the frame, and the face appears line by line. Viewers stay to see if it looks like the person.

The reveal is the payoff

The drawing turned toward the subject, and their reaction, is the moment people replay and share. Every video should end on it.

The audience is international

Subjects are tourists from everywhere, and comments arrive in English. The videos need almost no speech, so one channel can serve the whole world with bilingual titles.

Content theft is a real risk

Her pinned post warns that accounts are reposting her videos. YouTube's copyright tools give her stronger protection than Facebook does (see the Protection section).

Pillars

What she posts

Four content types. Shorts bring new viewers; long videos turn them into subscribers and earn ad revenue.

Live Draw Shorts 60%

The proven Reel format, cut to 30–58 seconds, vertical. Repost every Facebook Reel that did well, then post new ones daily.

Reaction Compilations 15%

8–12 minute long videos: "20 Strangers See Their Caricature". Built from existing reveal clips, so they cost almost nothing to make.

Full Sessions 15%

Uncut 10–20 minute drawings with ambient studio sound. Good for watch time; people play them in the background.

Behind the Board 10%

Studio day vlogs, Gamcheon Village walks, how-to-draw tutorials, a day at a corporate event. Shows her personality and brings in event bookings.

Shorts

How each Short is built

SecondsWhat's on screen
0–2The subject's face plus a text hook ("Will she like it?"). The first line of marker already on the paper. Never open on a blank page.
2–35Drawing, sped up 2–4×. Cut the slow parts. Keep the marker squeak audio or a light trending sound.
35–50The flip: board turns toward the subject. Hold on their reaction at normal speed.
50–58Side-by-side of face and drawing. End frame loops back into the start so viewers watch twice.
  • Ask permission from every subject before filming and posting; a small sign at the board ("Your drawing may appear on my channel") helps.
  • Pin a comment asking "Where should I draw next?" or "Rate this from 1–10" to get replies.
  • Link each Short to a related long video (YouTube Studio → Related video) to turn views into subscribers.

Protection

Stopping reposts

Content theft is already happening on Facebook. On YouTube she can catch it automatically.

  • Copyright Match Tool: becomes available once she joins the YouTube Partner Program. It finds full re-uploads of her videos on other channels and lets her request removal from YouTube Studio → Copyright.
  • Upload first on YouTube, or on the same day as Facebook, so YouTube holds her as the earliest source.
  • Visible handwriting watermark "@artjenny91" on every video.
  • Verified links: add the YouTube link to her Facebook and Instagram, and link both back from the channel, so fans can confirm the real account.
  • For reposts on Facebook, keep using Meta's IP report form (facebook.com/help/contact/634636770043106) with the YouTube link as proof of the original.
