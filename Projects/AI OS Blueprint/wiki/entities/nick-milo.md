# Nick Milo

**Who:** creator of Linking Your Thinking (YouTube channel "Linking Your Thinking with Nick Milo"), teacher of Obsidian-based personal knowledge management and author of the AI OS approach summarised in this project.

## From the sources
- Coined/uses: Ideaverse, ACE folders, maps of content, "file over AI", the Big Clean, IDI framework (see [[aios-system-overview]]).
- Vault of about 17,000 notes; uses Obsidian with Claude Cowork; writing a Linking Your Thinking book for spring 2027 (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]], [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]]).
- Paid products mentioned in the videos: Knowledge Academy, Obsidian Flight School, Obsidian Launchpad, Ideaverse Lite/Pro, Linking Your AI course. Several videos end with pitches for these; the method itself is explained in the videos.
- His beginner Obsidian masterclass is the foundation course; it mentions his earlier LYT Kit starter vault (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).
- Made the Soft Paper Obsidian theme (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-soft-paper-theme]]).
