# Video 2: "Give Me 20 Minutes. I'll Teach You 80% of Claude Cowork"

**What it is:** a ~20-minute walkthrough by [[nick-milo]] of using Claude Cowork (the desktop app mode that works on a chosen folder) as a productivity partner and thinking companion beside an Obsidian Ideaverse. Record: `raw/videos/2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80.md`.

## Key takeaways
1. Cowork sits between Chat and Code in the Claude desktop app: pick a folder, allow access, and Claude can read, rename, move and create files there without re-uploading or re-explaining ([[aios-claude-cowork]]).
2. Generic AI gives generic results. He had Cowork read his vault's Atlas notes and write a **dossier** of who he is (values, beliefs, frameworks, goals), then pasted it into Claude's personal-preferences setting; the results immediately fit him better ([[aios-me-md]]).
3. Worked example: ~324 unnamed screenshots → proposed categories (mapped onto ACE) → subfolders created, every file renamed with a **date prefix + short description**, plus a markdown index of each subfolder.
4. Turn a workflow you're happy with into a **skill** ("screenshot-renamer"), then **schedule** it (1st of every month, 9 am) so it runs in the background ([[aios-skills-as-files]]).
5. Linked notes are what make AI useful: research synthesis across a folder of 14 notes (common themes, where sources contradict *his* frameworks, insights for a curriculum), a "where am I / what matters / what am I missing" holistic briefing, adding an image to a note, and an AI-generated map of content surfacing buried notes for his book ([[aios-maps-of-content]]).

## Details worth keeping
- Point Claude at one subfolder by copying its path, so only those notes enter context.
- Watch the steps panel; stop and re-prompt if it drifts. Progress list shows multi-step work.
- Model choice: Sonnet first, Opus for heavy synthesis, Haiku only for trivial jobs ([[aios-model-choice]]).
- Mark AI-generated notes (he flags them in properties) and keep human judgement on top: AI surfaces tensions, it doesn't resolve them.
- Outputs saved as markdown in the vault are persistent, editable building blocks.
