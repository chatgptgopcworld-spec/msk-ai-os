# Video 5: "The Obsidian Theme I've Refined for 3 Years..."

**What it is:** [[nick-milo]] shows how to install and tune his Obsidian theme, Soft Paper. It is the workspace look of the system, not part of the AI logic. Record: `raw/videos/2026-10-22-nm-soft-paper-theme.md`.

## Key takeaways
1. Install: Settings → Appearance → Themes → Manage → search "Soft Paper". Preview themes with the command palette (Ctrl+P → "theme", arrow keys).
2. Character: warm paper-like background, serif body font, card-like writing area, coloured folders in the sidebar; light and dark modes (switch via the command palette). Optimised for light mode.
3. Tune it with the **Style Settings** community plugin → Soft Paper. His choices are recorded in [[aios-soft-paper-setup]].

## Details worth keeping
- On Obsidian 1.13+, turn off Settings → Interface → "open settings in new window" if you want the see-through settings panel.
- He shows the scroll bar again now that AI daily briefs make note length unpredictable.
