# Video 6: "My Complete Obsidian Masterclass (FULL GUIDE + SETUP)"

**What it is:** a ~40-minute beginner course by [[nick-milo]]: his earlier Obsidian-for-beginners series joined into one video. It teaches the foundations of layer 1 of the AI OS (the Ideaverse) rather than anything about AI. Record: `raw/videos/2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass.md`.

## Key takeaways
1. **A vault is just a folder; a note is just a `.md` plain-text file.** Readable by any text editor for as long as computers exist: the "future-proofing" that later becomes [[aios-file-over-ai]].
2. **Links are the core skill.** Type `[[` to link; an unresolved link is a placeholder for a note you may write later; backlinks ("linked mentions") show every note pointing here, with context; the graph view shows the web growing. Demo chain: a movie → an idea from it → a related idea → a book that idea reminded him of ([[aios-linking-your-thinking]]).
3. **Notes gain value over time.** Returning to a note and adding a new experience or connection makes it richer; each revisit is a "rep" that strengthens recall. Benefits: you can find and remember ideas, you build toward finished work (articles, talks ~80% done), and conversations get richer.
4. **Six markdown keys**, ranked by usefulness for thinking: link, tag, italic, bold, list, heading. Links are strong, direct relationships; tags are weak, loose ones. No formatting toolbar on purpose: ideas first, looks second ([[aios-obsidian-basics]]).
5. **Few settings and hotkeys matter** ([[aios-obsidian-basics]]). The one he calls most important: *automatically update internal links*, so renaming a note never breaks links to it.
6. **Closing message:** filling a vault with clipped articles and other people's words buries your own thinking. "Don't just take notes, make them."

## Details worth keeping
- Plugins he switches on: tag pane, page preview, starred notes (now called Bookmarks in current Obsidian).
- Deleted files: send to the system trash, so you always know where deletions go.
- Themes: community themes are fun but optional; then he preferred dark mode and his own colourful "Cybertron" theme. Video 5 shows he now prefers the light Soft Paper theme ([[aios-soft-paper-setup]]).
- Mentions his LYT Kit (starter vault) for structuring hundreds or thousands of notes, the forerunner of Ideaverse/ACE ([[aios-ace-framework]]).
- Hotkeys are given for Mac; on Windows use Ctrl for Cmd and Alt for Option.

## Questions it raises
- MSK AI OS's `.obsidian/app.json` has no explicit "automatically update internal links" setting, although the vault depends on links by unique file name. Worth turning on in Obsidian (see [[aios-msk-adoption-plan]]).
- The closing message ("make notes, don't just take them") cuts against a vault where AI writes all the wiki pages. See Synthesis on [[aios-linking-your-thinking]].
