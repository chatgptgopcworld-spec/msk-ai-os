# AI content hygiene and privacy

**Definition:** the habits that keep your own thinking distinct from AI output and keep private material under your control.

## What the sources say
- **Separate:** AI-generated material lives in the AIOS folder so it can be isolated or cleared, keeping the Ideaverse clean (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]).
- **Mark:** label AI-generated notes with an emoji, tag, property or folder so you always know what's yours (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-3-file-ai-system]]); he flags AI text in note properties and asked Cowork to mark a new MOC as AI-generated (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]]).
- **Limit access:** give AI only the folder it needs (copy a subfolder path); keep journals and other very personal areas out if you prefer (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]], [[src-2026-10-22-nm-3-file-ai-system]]).
- **Back up** always (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-3-file-ai-system]]).
- **Trust trade-off:** frontier tools mean trusting a vendor with data; he chose Claude because it says it doesn't train on user data and keeps data for a rolling 30 days; expects a move to local models later (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]). (His description; check Anthropic's current terms before relying on it.)
- **Human judgement stays on top:** AI surfaces tensions and options; you decide (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]]).

- His long-standing warning, from before AI: a vault filled with other people's words buries your own thinking; make notes, don't just take them (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).
