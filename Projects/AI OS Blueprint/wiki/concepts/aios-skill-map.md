# Skill map

**Definition:** the index of every skill in your AI OS: what each does, when AI should use it, and where its file is.

## What the sources say
- The third core file. Skills are listed in sections (briefings, email, files and maintenance, meetings, reviews, "chief of staff" thinking) (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-3-file-ai-system]]).
- In the later setup, skills are grouped by **system** (Auto Builder, Daily Trident, Sherpa, Weekly Review, Rock Tumbler, Chronicler, Janitor, Courier) (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]], [[aios-skill-systems]]).
- Gives "very clear instructions about what skills exist, what they do and exactly when AI should use them" (paraphrase of [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]).
