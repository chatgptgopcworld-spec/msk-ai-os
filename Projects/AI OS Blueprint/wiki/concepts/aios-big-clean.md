# The Big Clean

**Definition:** a periodic (he does it yearly) three-pass declutter: physical space → digital files → thinking space.

## What the sources say
1. **Physical:** clear the desk where most thinking happens; fewer distractions, calmer mind (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-big-clean]]).
2. **Digital:** sweep the desktop into one folder; set up the [[aios-plus-folder]] with single-source downloads and screenshots; size-sort and delete over 10 MB; archive by year with counts (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-big-clean]]).
3. **Thinking space (vault):** process the vault's `+` folder in bulk; batch-move obvious groups (people, one subject); for the rest, open notes, link them with `[[` autocomplete to related notes and collections, then file by context (e.g. Atlas) (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-big-clean]]).
- Follow-up: after the Big Clean, AI (Cowork) can finish the job: rename year folders of screenshots with date-prefixed descriptive names, sort into subfolders, and write an index (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]]).
