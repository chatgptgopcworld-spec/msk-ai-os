# Linking your thinking

**Definition:** Nick Milo's core practice: write ideas as your own notes and connect them with links, so they compound over time. The foundation the whole AI OS rests on.

## What the sources say
- Link from where the idea comes up; an unresolved link is a placeholder for a future note (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).
- Backlinks show every note that mentions this one, with surrounding text; the graph shows the web (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).
- Links come from *your* experience: a connection no source states (an idea reminding you of a book you read years ago) is the valuable part (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).
- A linked note grows in value as you revisit it and add to it; each visit is a rep that strengthens memory (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]], [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]).
- Payoffs: reliable recall, half-built articles and talks, richer conversations, new ideas across fields (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).
- Organise for context so a note can be found by many routes (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-big-clean]]).
- "Make notes, don't just take them": a vault of clipped articles and quotes buries your own thinking (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-masterclass]]).

## Synthesis
This is the strongest challenge the videos pose to MSK AI OS as built. The LLM Wiki pattern has Claude summarise sources into the wiki, which is very close to "filling the library with other people's words", only faster. The vault already protects the user's voice in one way (Claude's opinions sit under `## Synthesis`), but nowhere holds the user's *own* written thinking. A small personal-notes area that AI may read and link to but not rewrite would close the gap. See [[aios-msk-adoption-plan]], gap 6.
