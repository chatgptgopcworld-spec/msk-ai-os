# ACE folder framework

**Definition:** Nick Milo's top-level folders for an Obsidian Ideaverse: **A**tlas, **C**alendar, **E**fforts.

## What the sources say
- **Atlas**: knowledge, ideas, reference material, maps, people, statements; the things you want to think about and develop (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]).
- **Calendar**: time-based notes: daily notes, journals, meetings, anything tied to a moment (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]).
- **Efforts**: projects, tasks and works in progress; actions and outputs (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]]).
- Bonus folders: `+` for unsorted new notes ([[aios-plus-folder]]) and `X` (mentioned, not explained in these videos) (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-3-file-ai-system]]).
- The AIOS folder sits beside ACE, holding the AI maps (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-3-file-ai-system]]).
- Folders are "wayfinders" for AI; organisation is for context, so notes resurface when needed (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-big-clean]]).
- ACE is optional: start with whatever notes you have (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-obsidian-cowork-life]]).
- AI uses ACE to make better categories, e.g. sorting screenshots into knowledge assets / time-based / effort-based (see [[src-2026-10-22-nm-cowork-80]]).

Mapping to MSK AI OS: Atlas ≈ `wiki/` (concepts, entities, topics); Efforts ≈ `Projects/`; Calendar has no home yet (no daily notes or journal). `raw/inbox/` plays the `+` role for sources. Renaming folders isn't needed; the vault map just has to explain the mapping.
