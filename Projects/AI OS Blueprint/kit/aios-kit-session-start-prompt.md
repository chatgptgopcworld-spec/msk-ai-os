# Session-start prompt for MSK AI OS

Paste at the start of any AI session (or save as a text-expander snippet). Pattern from [[aios-session-start-prompt]], adapted to MSK AI OS's reading order.

Today (works now):
```text
You're working in my MSK AI OS vault (C:\Users\dell\Documents\MSK AI OS). First read CLAUDE.md at the vault root, then wiki/hot.md, then wiki/brain-index.md and the last 5 entries of wiki/brain-log.md. If I name a project, read Projects/<Name>/CLAUDE.md and its hot cache too. Confirm what you've read in two lines, then wait for my instruction.
```

After the AIOS folder exists (adoption plan steps 1-4):
```text
First read AIOS/msk-me.md in my MSK AI OS vault, then AIOS/msk-vault-map.md and AIOS/msk-skill-map.md, then wiki/hot.md. Confirm in two lines, then wait for my instruction.
```

Windows text-expansion options: espanso (free, open source), AutoHotkey hotstrings, or TextExpander.
