# Loophole audit (run before every delivery)

**Law**
- [ ] Every ingredient of every cited section has a supporting fact paragraph and, where possible, an exhibit (see the case's mapping table).
- [ ] Each section is current: section page `status` checked, [[legal-gaps]] checked, online check recorded where the source is outdated.
- [ ] No over-charging or mis-charging (a section cited without facts invites a [[ppc-s211]] allegation and weakens the rest).
- [ ] Right forum: territorial, subject-matter, pecuniary jurisdiction; PECA offences to [[nccia]] ([[peca-s30]]); prior remedy exhausted where required.
- [ ] Limitation checked, and any delay explained in the facts.

**Facts**
- [ ] Dates, times (PKT), names, spellings, CNIC, phone numbers, URLs and amounts identical everywhere.
- [ ] Chronological, numbered, one fact per paragraph; no conclusions dressed as facts.
- [ ] Nothing admits fault or contradicts another paragraph or an exhibit.
- [ ] Nothing overstated; unknowns in [BRACKETS] (see [[ppc-s182]]).
- [ ] Wording about the respondent stays within provable facts ("as per the message dated …", "allegedly"); no insults.

**Evidence** (see [[evidence-handling]])
- [ ] Originals preserved; nothing deleted; device available for examination.
- [ ] Screenshots show URL/profile, date and time; exports and hashes recorded; backup kept.
- [ ] Preservation request to the platform/telecom considered.
- [ ] Explicit or intimate material described, not attached.

**Prayer and reservations**
- [ ] Prayer specific and lawful, then "any other relief deemed just and proper".
- [ ] Right to supplement facts/evidence and to approach other forums reserved.
- [ ] Declaration/verification, enclosures, affidavit/stamp/fee notes as the forum requires.
- [ ] Third parties (family members, witnesses) mentioned only as needed and with their consent.
