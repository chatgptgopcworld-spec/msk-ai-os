# Evidence handling (electronic)

Admissibility and weight are for the court ([[qso-art164]] is discretionary). The complainant's job is to preserve evidence so its authenticity can be shown. Relevant provisions: [[qso-art46a]], [[qso-art73]], [[qso-art78a]], [[qso-art164]].

**Do**
- Keep the original device and account; do not delete chats, block-and-delete, or factory reset.
- Take screenshots that show the profile/number/URL, the date and the time; then also export the chat (WhatsApp "Export chat", platform data download) where possible.
- Save the post/profile URL and the account's numeric ID if visible.
- Record a hash of each exported file and keep it in the evidence list, e.g. on Windows `certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256`.
- Keep two backups (e.g. external drive + cloud) and do not edit the files.
- Write a short contemporaneous note for each item: what it is, when and how it was captured, by whom.
- Ask the platform or telecom to preserve data where a route exists; investigators can use PECA s.31 (expedited preservation, not yet extracted).

**Don't**
- Don't crop, annotate or "enhance" the copy you will submit (keep any marked-up copy separate and labelled).
- Don't publish the material or the allegations online while the complaint is pending (see [[peca-s26a]], [[ppc-s499]]).
- Don't attach intimate or explicit images to a written complaint; describe them and give them to the investigator directly.
