# National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA)

## Legal basis
- Established under [[peca-s29]] (substituted by Act II of 2025). Headed by a Director General; its officers are deemed police officers of equivalent rank under the Cr.P.C. for inquiry and investigation.
- [[peca-s29]](4): the FIA Cyber Crime Wing ceased to exist and its personnel, cases and inquiries transferred to NCCIA. **Address complaints to NCCIA, not "FIA Cyber Crime Wing".**
- [[peca-s30]]: only an authorized officer of the investigation agency may investigate PECA offences (joint investigation teams allowed).
- Complaint registration procedure: [[peca-rules-r6]] (Rules of 2018, written for the FIA Cybercrime Wing; whether NCCIA has its own notified rules is open, see [[legal-gaps]]).

## Subject-matter jurisdiction
Offences under PECA 2016 (e.g. [[peca-s14]], [[peca-s16]], [[peca-s20]], [[peca-s21]], [[peca-s24]]). Non-PECA offences arising from the same incident (e.g. a physical threat at the door) may need the police route: [[police-fir]].

## How to file (from NCCIA's own site, checked 2026-10-22)
- Online portal: **complaint.nccia.gov.pk**
- Helpline: **1799** (24/7)
- Email: **helpdesk@nccia.gov.pk**
- HQ: 2nd Floor, National Police Foundation Building, Sector G-10/4, Islamabad
- Regional Cyber Crime Reporting Centres: list at nccia.gov.pk ("NCCIA offices" page). Look up the nearest centre at filing time rather than relying on a stored address.
- Reported Oct 2026 (APP): police emergency 15 systems in several provinces/cities are linked to NCCIA so cyber complaints received on 15 are forwarded. Treat this as a supplementary channel, not a substitute for a written complaint.

## What to keep
Complaint/reference number, date and time of submission, a copy of exactly what was submitted, and every acknowledgement. The NCCIA reference is asked for by banks, the Banking Mohtasib and PTA in related complaints.

## Escalation if no action (needs-check)
Written reminder to the circle/centre in-charge quoting the reference → Director of the zone → DG NCCIA (dg@nccia.gov.pk per NCCIA site) → court remedies (to be confirmed against current Cr.P.C. and an advocate's advice; see [[crpc-1898]]).

## Content removal
Removal/blocking applications under PECA ss.20(2), 21(3), 24(3) go to "the Authority": see [[smpra]].
