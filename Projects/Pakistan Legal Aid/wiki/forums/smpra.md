# Social Media Protection and Regulatory Authority (SMPRA), "the Authority" under PECA

## Legal basis
- PECA s.2(iv) defines "Authority" as the SMPRA established under s.2A (Chapter 1A, inserted by Act II of 2025).
- s.2C: a person aggrieved by fake and false information may apply to the Authority for removal or blocking; the Authority is to pass orders within 24 hours.
- Removal/blocking applications by victims: [[peca-s16]](2), [[peca-s20]](2), [[peca-s21]](3), [[peca-s24]](3), [[peca-s24a]](2).
- PECA also provides a Social Media Complaint Council and Social Media Protection Tribunals.

## Current status (needs re-checking each time)
- Constituted in March 2026; chairman and members appointed (The News, 20 Mar 2026).
- ProPakistani reported on 21 July 2026 that the SMPRA had **not begun operations** (no budget, office, staff or secretariat yet) and that the **tribunal had not been established**.
- Practical consequence: before advising a removal application, check whether SMPRA is accepting applications and through what channel. Until it is, record the gap in the case file and ask the platform to remove the content through its own reporting tools, while preserving evidence first ([[evidence-handling]]).
