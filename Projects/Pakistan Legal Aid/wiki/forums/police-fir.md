# Police: FIR, and the Justice of the Peace route (Cr.P.C. s.154, s.22-A/22-B)

> [!warning] Cr.P.C. not yet in raw/
> Section references below come from High Court orders, not from a verified text of the Code. Ingest a current Cr.P.C. ([[crpc-1898]]) and re-check before citing.

**Emergency first:** if there is danger to life or safety, call **15** or go to the nearest police station before any drafting.

## When this route applies
- Cognizable offences under the PPC and other laws (cognizability is decided by Cr.P.C. Schedule II, not yet ingested).
- Not for PECA offences: [[peca-s30]] gives investigation to the NCCIA ([[nccia]]).

## Steps
1. **Report to the SHO** of the police station with territorial jurisdiction over the place of occurrence (s.154). Get a copy of the FIR or, if refused, keep a dated copy of the written application with a receipt/diary number.
2. **If the police refuse or delay:** application under **s.22-A(6)(i) Cr.P.C.** to the **ex-officio Justice of the Peace** (the Sessions Judge / Additional Sessions Judge) for a direction to register the case. As the Sindh High Court has put it, a police officer's failure or delay in registering a cognizable complaint is a failure of statutory duty that can be taken to the Justice of the Peace.
3. **Other options at the same stage:** a private complaint before a Magistrate under **s.200 Cr.P.C.**; requests to higher police officers. Courts have treated these alternatives as relevant when deciding 22-A applications.
4. **If the Justice of the Peace refuses:** a petition under **s.561-A Cr.P.C.** in the High Court (advocate needed).

## What the Justice of the Peace looks at (from SHC orders)
- The function is quasi-judicial and the power is discretionary; there is no duty to direct registration in every case.
- They may refuse where no cognizable offence appears, where the application looks mala fide or like a counterblast, or where earlier applications were concealed.
- They do not conduct a detailed fact-finding inquiry.
- A safeguard against false complaints is [[ppc-s182]].

**Drafting consequences:** attach proof that the police were approached first (dated application, receipt); disclose any earlier application or case; state facts showing each ingredient of a cognizable offence; avoid language suggesting a counterblast.
