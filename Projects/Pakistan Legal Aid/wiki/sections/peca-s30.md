## Drafting notes (Claude's breakdown, not law)

Only an authorized officer of the investigation agency (NCCIA) may investigate PECA offences, subject to joint investigation teams. Practical effect: PECA offences go to [[nccia]], not to the local police station; non-PECA offences in the same incident (e.g. physical threats) may still need the police route ([[police-fir]]).
