# PPC s.500 Punishment for defamation

Statute: [[ppc-1860]]

> [!warning] Source currency
> This text is from a compilation amended up to 14 Dec 2023. Later amendments exist (see [[legal-gaps]]). Confirm this section is unchanged before citing it.

## Verbatim text

```text
500. Punishment for defamation. Whoever defames another shall be punished with simple
imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both 2[.]
3[Provided that the originator of the defamatory imputation shall be punished with imprisonment

of either description for a term which may extend to five years, or with fine which shall not be less
than one hundred thousand rupees, or with both.
Explanation .
2*

“Originator” means the initiator of a defamatory imputation.]
```
