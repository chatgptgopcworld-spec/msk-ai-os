# QSO Art.46-A Relevance of information generated, received or recorded by automated information system

Statute: [[qso-1984]]

## Verbatim text

```text
[46-A. Relevance of information generated, received or recorded by automated
information system. — Statements in the form of electronic documents generated, received or
recorded by an automated information system while it is in working order, are relevant facts.]
```
