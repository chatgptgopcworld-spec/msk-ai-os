# PECA s.14 Electronic fraud

Statute: [[peca-2016]]

## Verbatim text

```text
14. Electronic fraud.—Whoever with the intent for wrongful gain interferes with or uses
any information system, device or data or induces any person to enter into a relationship or
deceives any person, which act or omission is likely to cause damage or harm to that person or
any other person shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years
or with fine which may extend to ten million rupees or with both.
```
