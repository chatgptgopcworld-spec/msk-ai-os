# QSO Art.78-A Proof of electronic signature and electronic document

Statute: [[qso-1984]]

## Verbatim text

```text
[78-A. Proof of electronic signature and electronic document. — If an electronic
document is alleged to be signed or to have been generated wholly or in part by any person through the
use of an information system, and where such allegation is denied, the application of a security procedure
to the signature or the electronic document must be proved.]
```
