# Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (Act XL of 2016)

**Source used:** [[source-01-peca-sja]] (consolidated up to Act II of 2025). Cross-check only: [[source-02-peca-bill-2025-na]], [[source-03-cla-2023-pta]].

## Amendment history in the sources
- **Act XXXVII of 2023** (Criminal Laws (Amendment) Act 2023, assented 18 July 2023): per footnotes in source 01, inserted s.24A (cyberbullying) and ss.30A-30D (remand, victim/witness protection, in-camera trial, child sexual content), among others.
- **Act II of 2025** (dated 29 January 2025, per footnotes in source 01): inserted s.26A (false and fake information); substituted s.29 (creates the NCCIA) and s.30 (power to investigate); added Chapter 1A creating the Social Media Protection and Regulatory Authority ([[smpra]]), the Social Media Complaint Council and Social Media Protection Tribunals.
- Anything after January 2025: not in the sources. Check before filing.
