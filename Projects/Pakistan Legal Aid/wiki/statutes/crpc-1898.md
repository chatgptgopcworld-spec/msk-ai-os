# Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act V of 1898)

> [!danger] No source in raw/

Note: the Criminal Laws (Amendment) Act 2025 (Act XXVII of 2025) amended Schedule II, so any copy older than Sept 2025 is out of date for the schedule.
