# Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, 1973

**Source used:** [[source-07-constitution-na]], as modified up to 22 Dec 2017.

> [!warning] Do not cite from this copy
> Missing at least the **26th Amendment (2024)** and the **27th Amendment (2025)**. The 27th Amendment, passed by the Senate on 10 Nov 2025 and the National Assembly on 12 Nov 2025, created a **Federal Constitutional Court** and amended Articles including 199 and 200; reporting indicates the FCC takes over constitutional jurisdiction formerly with the Supreme Court, including certain appeals in Article 199 matters ([APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1108126), [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/1954264), checked 2026-10-22). Any writ-petition route must be checked against the current text and an advocate's advice.

No section pages are extracted from this source on purpose. Add a current official copy (na.gov.pk or pakistancode.gov.pk) before relying on Articles 4, 9, 10A, 14, 19, 19A, 25 or 199.
