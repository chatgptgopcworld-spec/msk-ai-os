How the WhatsApp column works

Each row has a button. It opens WhatsApp with the candidate's number and the right message already written (Shortlist, Maybe or Reject). You read it, press Send. Nothing goes out without you pressing Send.

Change the interview details and messages below, then press Save changes. They are saved to the Templates tab of your Google Sheet, so the WhatsApp buttons in the Sheet use them too.

Edit any message above. The WhatsApp buttons in the table above use your edited text straight away. {name} {date} {time} {address} {contact} are filled per candidate. The Reminder message also fills {day} (today / tomorrow / on date) and {link} (the candidate's private Yes / No link).
